# Study Map: Claude Certified Architect – Foundations (CCAR-F)

## Exam basics
- **Items:** 60, multiple-choice and multiple-response. Each item states how many responses to select.
- **Time:** 120 minutes. *Estimate: about 2 minutes per item.*
- **Passing score:** 720 on a scaled range of 100–1,000. The cut score came from a standard-setting study.
- **Scenarios:** 4 are drawn at random from a bank of 6, and the exam is built around them.
- **Score report:** Pass/fail, scaled score, and percent correct by domain. Domain percentages do not decide pass/fail.
- **Other facts:** Proctored, $125 fee, 12-month validity. Retake waits are 14, 30, and 90 days, with up to 4 attempts in a rolling 12 months.

## Domains (heaviest first)

## What the guide doesn't say
- How the 60 items split between multiple-choice and multiple-response, or how multiple-response items are scored (partial credit or all-or-nothing).
- Which items belong to which domain or scenario. The weights are approximate, and per-scenario item counts are not given.
- Any minimum score per domain, or how the 720 cut score maps to percent correct.
- Time allocation across items or scenarios, or whether the 120 minutes includes the agreement and check-in steps.
- The domain tags on the 12 sample questions. The guide presents them as practice-test items, not as a representative slice of the real exam.
- Which product versions or API details the exam reflects beyond the July 2026 guide, or how often the guide itself changes.
